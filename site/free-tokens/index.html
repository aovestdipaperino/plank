Terms and conditions. Ten million tokens is an arbitrary number chosen because it sounds generous. The actual figure is “as many as you like”, which tested poorly in focus groups — nobody believes a free thing that admits it is unlimited.

Tokens are generated on your own machine by a model you downloaded once, using electricity you were already buying. Plank does not meter them, count them, or send them anywhere. The /usage command will happily bill you in imaginary currency if you miss the ritual.

Offer void where a GPU is absent. Offer also void on Linux and Windows, not out of spite but out of Metal. Your tokens may be smaller than they appear; a token is roughly three quarters of a word, and nobody has ever been happy about that.

By reading this page you agree that “free” meaning free is a strange enough claim in 2026 to warrant a whole page about it.
